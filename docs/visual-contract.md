# Visual contract

Editorial research case study matching the approved research-report render. White/off-white paper, deep ink and teal, restrained Georgia publication headings and abstract, system sans-serif analytical labels and controls. Figures and ruled sections replace dashboard cards. No em dashes, invented metrics, maps, decorative badges, employer targeting or academic credentials.

Opening: clear research question, short abstract, study scope and held-out model comparison. Four numbered sections: evidence with a shared-scale count figure and interpretation; methods table and chronological evaluation; exact results with review coverage and discussion; live fixed-example prototype. Source, reproducibility, methodological choices, category failures, limitations and contribution disclosures stay accessible.

Desktop uses balanced publication columns with fine dividers. Mobile stacks figures and text without shrinking labels, maintains 44px controls and keyboard focus, and confines horizontal scrolling to evidence tables. Normal scrolling and reduced-motion support. Trend disclosure opens when a finding is selected. The demo runs the existing safe model locally, with neutral cutoff labels and an explicit mistake example.

All values derive from the frozen reports. Counts describe the selected sample, not customer failure rates. Accepted accuracy and coverage appear together with exact denominators. Research references inform methods but do not validate this project's results. Operational improvements remain proposed pilots. Raw narratives stay private; no arbitrary visitor text or uploads.
