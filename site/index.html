Know what this evidence can say

Scope and limits

No customer exposure denominator, causal identification, satisfaction measure, savings, adoption, or Arabic/Japanese benchmark. Timely response is an administrative measure, not resolution.

Monthly sampling is unweighted. Trends describe this sample; six months cannot establish seasonality. Near-duplicate screening is approximate and can miss paraphrases. Usability under one minute is a target, not a measured result.
